Foundey UX Challenge

My thought process

How I approached a high-volume fulfillment operations workflow.

01

What problem I am solving

I am reducing the cognitive load operators face when work is split across orders, notifications, communications, and recent activity. At the same time, I am giving managers a clear view of team allocation without crowding the operator’s workspace.

02

What I prioritized and why

I prioritized time-sensitive work and the next clear action. A single queue sorted by SLA risk makes urgency explicit, while task details stay scannable.

  • Show urgency as text, icon, and color—not color alone.
  • Keep operator and manager needs in distinct views.
  • Make key actions reachable by keyboard and touch.
03

My proposed solution

I designed a responsive operations control center around one ranked queue. Operators can filter, search, and claim work in place. Managers can switch to a live allocation view, and reports and settings remain one navigation action away.

04

How I measure success

I would validate the solution with lower time-to-next-task, fewer missed SLAs, improved resolution time, and positive operator feedback. I would also test manager confidence in understanding workload distribution across the shift.
